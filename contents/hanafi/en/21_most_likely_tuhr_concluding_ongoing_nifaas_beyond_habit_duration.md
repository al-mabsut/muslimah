# Ruling: Most Likely Tuhr (Concluding Ongoing Nifaas beyond Habit Duration of a Previous Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Since bleeding has completely stopped (no spotting), perform Ghusl and resume Salah.

## Additional Clarifications

- Record your Ghusl date/time.
- Your bleeding ended beyond your previous pregnancy’s habit duration.
- Nifaas can last up to 40 days (960 hours). Even if it stops early, it may resume:
  - If it does, any Salah/fasting you performed will be invalidated.
- If bleeding goes beyond 40 days, then your habit will apply:
  - Only bleeding up to your habit duration counts as Nifaas.
  - Any bleeding after that is Istihadah, requiring Qadaa for Salah/fasting performed during this period. You will not be sinful since you followed Nifaas rules.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Religiously speaking, you are to fast unless it risks harming you or your child, especially if you are breastfeeding.

## Marital Clarifications

- Conjugal relationships are allowed as long as bleeding has not resumed.
