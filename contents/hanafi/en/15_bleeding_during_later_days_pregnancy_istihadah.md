# Ruling: Istihadah (Bleeding During Later Days of Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Treat this bleeding as Istihadah.

## Additional Clarifications

- You are far enough into pregnancy that this bleeding cannot become Hayd later on.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- You are not required to fast during pregnancy, if you fear for your health or the health of the fetus.
- However, if you have decided to fast under consultation of a doctor, then from a religious standpoint, you can fast normally, ignoring this bleeding.

## Marital Clarifications

- Conjugal relations are allowed.
- Divorce during pregnancy is binding, and ‘iddat ends upon delivery of the baby.
