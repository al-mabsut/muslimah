# Ruling: Most Likely Tuhr (Concluding Ongoing Nifaas within Habit Duration of a Previous Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Since bleeding has completely stopped (no spotting), perform Wajib Ghusl and resume Salah.
- Perform Mustahabb Ghusl at the end of your habit duration of your previous pregnancy.

## Additional Clarifications

- Record your Wajib Ghusl date/time.
- Your bleeding ended within your previous pregnancy’s habit duration.
- Nifaas can last up to 40 days (960 hours). Even if it stops early, it may resume:
  - If it does, any Salah/fasting you performed will be invalidated.
- If bleeding goes beyond 40 days, then your habit will apply:
  - Only bleeding up to your habit duration counts as Nifaas.
  - Any bleeding after that is Istihadah, requiring Qadaa for Salah/fasting performed during this period. You will not be sinful since you followed Nifaas rules.
- The Mustahabb Ghusl at the end of your habit duration of your previous pregnancy is to protect you from your Wajib Ghusl to get invalidated by your Nifaas, thereby potentially affecting any of your worship at the conclusion of your "Cautionary Period".
  - This is a complex topic outside the scope of this guidance modal:
    - We strongly advise you to undertake coursework to properly understand this topic.
    - Consult a scholar for more information.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Religiously speaking, you are to fast unless it risks harming you or your child, especially if you are breastfeeding.

## Marital Clarifications

- While your previous habit duration still applies:
  - The husband must not see or touch your skin between navel and knee.
  - Any vaginal stimulation is forbidden (including intercourse).
  - Divorce is not allowed, but if issued, it will be valid and carry its full implications.
- Permitted contact includes:
  - Resting his head on the wife’s clothed lap.
  - Kissing (including mouth-to-mouth).
  - Fondling breasts or other non-vaginal contact.
- Normal activities (eating together, sharing a bed, etc.) are allowed.
