# Ruling: Most Likely Tuhr (Concluding Ongoing Nifaas of First-Time Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Since bleeding fully stops (no spotting), then Ghusl is Wajib and you are to resume your Salah.

## Additional Clarifications

- Record the date/time of your Ghusl.
- Nifaas can last up to 40 days (960 hours). Even though your bleeding has stopped early, it may resume before 40 days end:
  - If it does, any Salah/fasting you performed will be invalidated; even though you were advised to perform fasting/Salah.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Religiously speaking, you are to fast unless it risks harming you or your child, especially if you are breastfeeding.

## Marital Clarifications

- For the first 40 days after childbirth (of a first pregnancy):
  - The husband must not see or touch your skin between navel and knee.
  - Any vaginal stimulation is forbidden (including intercourse).
- Permitted contact includes:
  - Resting his head on the wife’s clothed lap.
  - Kissing (including mouth-to-mouth).
  - Fondling breasts or other non-vaginal contact.
- Normal activities (eating together, sharing a bed, etc.) are allowed.
- Divorce is not allowed, but if issued, it will be valid and carry its full implications.
