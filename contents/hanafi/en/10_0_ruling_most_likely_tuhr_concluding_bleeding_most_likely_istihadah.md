- Your Early Bleeding has stopped before completing 72 hours.

## Additional Clarifications

- The official guidance of Early Bleeding is that you are to treat it as Istihadah, and only start treating your bleeding as Hayd, once you have reached your Hayd Zone.
- You are currently waiting for the following future possibilities to unfold:
  - Either, your bleeding will resume at some point before your Hayd Zone. All such bleeding will become discounted as Early Bleeding
  - Either, your bleeding will resume at some point inside your Hayd Zone. Such bleeding is to be regarded as Hayd-bleeding, requiring you to abstain from worship.

In such a case, your Early Bleeding will change into Hayd, provided it bridges 3 days (72 hours) or more. Since you were instructed to continue all your worship as normal, any Qadhaa Salaah offered and any Ramadan fasting performed here will need to be repeated. You will not be sinful for having performed your worship during these days, as it only became Hayd in retrospect. You acted on the best evidence available to you at the time.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Regardless as to the natural properties of Early Bleeding, any such bleeding is to be ignored and you should continue fasting.
- So whether your bleeding ended in the middle of the day or at night, in all such cases you are required to continue fasting.
