# Ruling: Most Likely Istihadah (Bleeding During Initial Days of Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Keep tracking all discharges as usual.
- While pregnant, any bleeding is treated as Istihadah.

## Additional Points

- In Hanafi Madhhab, Hayd cannot occur during pregnancy and should be disregarded.
- Exception: If the pregnancy ends before the fetus develops features like hair, nails, or fingers, that bleeding can count as Hayd.
- If a pregnancy ends before 120 days, consult a scholar to confirm if the bleeding is Hayd or Istihadah.

## Ramadan

- You are not required to fast during pregnancy, if you fear for your health or the health of the fetus.
- However, if you have decided to fast under consultation of a doctor, then from a religious standpoint, you can fast normally, ignoring this bleeding.
- If a miscarriage occurs before 120 days, check with a scholar to see if the bleeding was retroactive Hayd. If so, then any fasts that fell on those days are subject to Qadaa.

## Marital Issues

- Conjugal relations are permitted as usual.
- Divorce is binding during pregnancy:
  - If a miscarriage happens before 120 days, consult a scholar to determine the end of ‘iddat.
  - If the pregnancy goes beyond 120 days, ‘iddat ends at delivery, whether by miscarriage or a normal birth.
