# Ruling: Ongoing Nifaas (First-Time Pregnancy)

## Guidance

- Do not fast or perform Salah while bleeding continues (spotting counts as bleeding).
- Once bleeding fully stops (no spotting), then Ghusl is Wajib before resuming Salah.
- If you are unsure whether bleeding will restart, delay your prayer until the last permissible time:
  - Fajr and Dhuhr: Pray at the last possible moment.
  - ‘Asr, Maghrib, and ‘Isha: Pray before Makrooh time, defined as:
    - ‘Asr: About 20 minutes before sunset (when the sun turns pale yellow).
    - Maghrib: When many stars are visible, but with Western horizon still red.
    - ‘Isha: At the midpoint between Maghrib and Fajr for men able to attend Salaah in congregation. For women, this does not apply so they will complete ‘Isha at the last possible moment.

## Additional Clarifications

- You mentioned this is your first pregnancy’s Nifaas. If it is not, adjust this information.
- If your bleeding has stopped for good, submit it as “Final Bleeding” so that guidance aligns with your actual condition.
- Nifaas can last up to 40 days (960 hours) after childbirth. Any bleeding during this period invalidates fasting/Salah you performed in that time.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Fasting is not allowed during Nifaas.
- If bleeding stops, ensure fasting will not harm you or your child. Breastfeeding can be demanding, so you may delay fasting if it poses a risk.

## Marital Clarifications

- Physical boundaries for the husband:
  - He must avoid seeing or touching the wife’s skin between the navel and knee.
  - Any act that stimulates the wife’s vagina is prohibited (including intercourse or manual stimulation).
- Allowed physical contact:
  - Resting his head on the wife’s clothed lap.
  - Kissing (including mouth-to-mouth).
  - Fondling breasts or other non-vaginal contact.
- Husbands and wives can still do normal daily activities together (eating, sleeping in the same bed, etc.).
- During the first 40 days after childbirth:
  - Sexual intercourse is not allowed, even if bleeding stops earlier.
  - Divorce is not allowed, but if issued, it will be valid and carry its full implications.
