# Ruling: Ongoing Nifaas (Within Habit Duration)

## Guidance

- Do not pray or fast while bleeding continues (even spotting).
- Once bleeding fully stops (no spotting), perform Ghusl before resuming Salah.
- If you are unsure whether bleeding will resume, pray at the last permissible time:
  - **Fajr & Dhuhr**: Just before each time ends.
  - **‘Asr, Maghrib, ‘Isha**: Before “Makrooh” time begins:
    - ‘Asr ~ 20 minutes before sunset.
    - Maghrib ~ When the sky still shows some light, though stars are visible.
    - ‘Isha ~ Midpoint between Maghrib and Fajr for men in congregation; for women, the last permissible moment.

## Additional Clarifications

- Your Nifaas remains within your previous pregnancy’s habit duration.
- If bleeding has definitively stopped, submit “Final Bleeding” so guidance can be updated.
- Nifaas lasts up to 40 days (960 hours) after childbirth:
  - If fewer than 40 days have passed, you still have Nifaas and cannot fast or pray.
  - If more than 40 days have passed, your Nifaas follows your past habit:
    - Bleeding within this habit period invalidates any fasting/Salah you performed.
    - Bleeding after this habit period is Istihadah. You must make up (Qadaa) any missed prayers, but you incur no sin since you followed Nifaas rules.
- Note how long this Nifaas lasts, as it affects future pregnancies.

## Ramadan Clarifications

- Fasting during Nifaas is prohibited.
- If bleeding stops, make sure fasting won’t harm you or your child. Postpone if there is a risk, especially while breastfeeding.

## Marital Clarifications

- From childbirth until your previous Nifaas habit ends:
  - Physical boundaries apply, even if bleeding stops sooner.
  - Divorce is not permitted, but if issued, it remains valid with full effects.
- Husband must avoid seeing or touching the wife’s skin between navel and knee.
- Any act that stimulates the wife’s vagina is prohibited (including intercourse).
- Allowed physical contact:
  - Resting his head on the wife’s clothed lap
  - Kissing (including mouth-to-mouth)
  - Fondling breasts or non-vaginal contact
- Normal daily interactions (eating, sleeping together, etc.) remain permitted.
